## The one rule for Day 2 onward

`ground_truth.csv` exists **only** to score your detector after the fact.
Graph construction, community detection, and feature engineering must never
read `ring_id`, `is_ring_member`, or `coincidental_group_id`. If any of
those three columns end up inside your feature matrix, your precision/recall
numbers are meaningless.

## Re-running / changing parameters

All tunable knobs are the uppercase constants at the top of
`generate_synthetic_data.py` (`N_LEGIT`, `N_RINGS`, ring size range,
coincidental group settings, date range). Same seed → same data, so re-run
freely while you're still deciding on population size.
